Human Boundaries & Pillars

Technology as a Silent Mediator

Place the device flat on the table, enter Table Mode, and look into each other's eyes while talking. The software stays quietly in the background.

Zero Biometric AI & No Voice Cloning

The human voice is treated as a sacred, irreproducible treasure. Strictly zero voice cloning, synthesis, AI transcription APIs, or biometric cloud uploads. 100% offline.

Sealed Time-Capsule (.echoes)

Encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305 and a key derived from your password with Argon2id (libsodium), with Zstandard level 9 compression for metadata. Keys live in protected memory and are wiped after use.

Open Capsule 40+ Years Longevity

Export an autonomous, self-contained HTML player alongside standard Ogg Opus audio. Works directly via file:// in any browser without internet or external servers for decades.

Zero Gamification & Gentle Decks

Curated invitation card decks across 10 languages. Any question can be passed gently without pressure or guilt. Zero completion percentages or anxiety loops.

Cross-Platform Android & Desktop

Built with Compose Multiplatform for Android, Windows, and Linux. Full visual and cryptographic parity between your phone and your desktop computer.

Privacy Policy & Microphone Usage (RECORD_AUDIO)

1. Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO)

Echoes of Them requests access to your microphone strictly when you deliberately tap to record an oral memory or interview in Table Mode. The microphone is never activated in the background or without your explicit, visible consent.

2. Strictly Local Storage & Zero Transmission

All audio recordings are encoded into standard RFC 7845 Ogg Opus files and stored solely within your device's private sandbox (and sealed within your encrypted .echoes capsules). No voice recordings are ever uploaded to cloud servers, shared with third parties, or used for biometric profiling.

3. User Sovereignty & Data Deletion

You retain complete custody of your archive: you can revoke microphone access at any time through system settings and permanently delete any voice recording or person profile directly inside the application.

Help Center & Archival FAQ

What is the .echoes sealed capsule format?

The .echoes format is a sovereign, tamper-resistant archive. It packs metadata, person profiles, waveform peaks, and audio tracks, compresses the metadata with Zstandard level 9, and encrypts everything with XChaCha20-Poly1305 using a key derived from your master password with Argon2id. Any tampering is detected, and only the master password can open it.

How does the 40+ year HTML Player export work?

When exporting an open capsule, the app produces a self-contained index.html alongside pure .opus files. It contains embedded CSS and vanilla JavaScript with zero CDN or external dependencies, opening directly via file:// in any browser today or decades from now.

What is Table Mode?

Table Mode is a dual-orientation interface designed for intimacy. Place the phone flat between two people: one side displays the invitation card right-side up for the speaker, while the other side allows subtle control without taking attention away from eye contact.

Does Echoes of Them use AI to synthesize or clone voices?

Never. Echoes of Them has a strict philosophical boundary: zero voice cloning, zero generative AI, and zero biometric profiling. Audio recorded is treated with sacred respect and remains 100% human and unmanipulated.

How does the Desktop version work (Windows & Linux)?

Echoes of Them Desktop provides full parity with mobile: offline architecture, native C++17 security bindings, full .echoes capsule compatibility, and comfortable long-form listening on your PC.
